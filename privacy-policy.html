🔒 Privacy Policy

MDMS Mobile Application

Last Updated: September 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the MDMS Mobile ("MDMS Mobile", "we", "our", or "us") application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

MDMS Mobile is a modern document management system companion app designed to deliver real-time push notifications and document tracking updates to authorized personnel.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

a. Authentication Information

  • Email address — Used to authenticate your identity and log you into the system.
  • Password — Transmitted securely to our server for authentication. Passwords are never stored on the device.

b. Device Information

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token — A unique device identifier generated by Google Firebase, used solely to deliver push notifications to your device.

c. Application Data

  • Authentication token — Stored locally on your device to maintain your login session.
  • Notification data — Document titles, messages, timestamps, and related metadata received from the server.
Data Type Purpose Storage
Email & Password User authentication Server only
FCM Device Token Push notifications Server & device
Auth Session Token Maintain login session Device only
Notification Content Display document updates Server (transmitted to device)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Authentication — To verify your identity and provide access to the application.
  • Push Notifications — To send you timely updates about documents that require your attention.
  • Session Management — To keep you logged in between app sessions for convenience.
  • Document Updates — To display relevant document information and notifications within the app.

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose other than the core functionality of the application.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as described below:

  • Google Firebase — We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications. Firebase may process your FCM device token in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

No other third-party services have access to your data.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using secure shared preferences.
  • Your password is transmitted to our secure server for verification and is never stored on the device.
  • All communication between the app and our servers is conducted over secure channels.
  • FCM tokens are stored on our server solely for the purpose of sending push notifications.

6. Data Retention

  • Your authentication session data is stored on the device until you log out.
  • Upon logging out, the locally stored authentication token is immediately deleted from the device.
  • FCM tokens stored on our server are associated with your account and are updated when the device token changes.
  • Server-side data is retained in accordance with applicable government data retention policies.

7. Your Rights

As a user of this application, you have the right to:

  • Access — Request information about the personal data we hold about you.
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting your LGU administrator.
  • Withdraw Consent — You may stop using the application at any time and revoke notification permissions through your device settings.
  • Logout — You may log out of the application at any time, which will remove your session data from the device.

8. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for authorized government personnel only and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
