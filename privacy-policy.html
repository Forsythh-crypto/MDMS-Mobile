Privacy Policy

Document Management System

Version 2.0 • Last Updated: September 14, 2026

1 Introduction

Welcome to the Document Management System ("we", "our", or "us") mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, and safeguard your personal information when utilizing our mobile companion services.

Document Management System is an enterprise and institutional companion app developed to deliver real-time push notifications, secure document tracking, and routing updates directly to authorized personnel.

2 Information We Collect

We collect only the essential data required to securely operate the system and deliver alerts:

a. Authentication Information

  • Email Address & User ID — Used to authenticate your account and securely regulate access.
  • Password — Transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS to our authentication endpoint. Passwords are never stored on your physical device.

b. Device Identifiers

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Token — A unique device token generated by Google Firebase, used solely to route incoming document notifications to your mobile device.

c. Session & Application State

  • Encrypted Session Token — Stored locally on your device via secure device storage to maintain seamless access without requiring re-login on every launch.
  • Notification Metadata — Document reference numbers, titles, timestamps, and status payloads dispatched by the backend server.
Data Category Purpose Storage Location
Email & Password Identity Verification Encrypted Server-Side Only
FCM Device Token Push Notification Routing Server & Firebase Infrastructure
Session Token Persistent Authentication Local Secure Device Storage
Notification Payloads Document Tracking & Inbox Central Database & Secure Push

3 How We Utilize Information

We process collected information strictly for operational and security functionality:

  • Access Control: Verifying credentials against the authorized personnel directory.
  • Real-Time Alerts: Dispatching push notifications when documents are assigned, received, or acted upon.
  • Session Integrity: Securing access sessions and validating API requests.
  • Document Lifecycle Tracking: Providing authorized timeline inspection and audit compliance.

🛡️ Zero Advertising / Analytics Policy: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or utilize user data for commercial advertising, behavioral tracking, or data profiling.

4 Third-Party Service Providers

We do not share your information with third-party vendors except for essential infrastructure services:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: Utilized for dispatching cloud notifications. Firebase handles device registration tokens under Google's Privacy & Terms.

5 Data Storage & Security Standards

  • All network communications are transmitted exclusively over industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption.
  • Local authentication credentials are encrypted using secure Android Keystore / iOS Keychain shared storage.
  • Account credentials and document payloads reside within protected government enterprise database servers.

6 Data Retention & Account Deletion

  • Device Cleanup: Logging out immediately purges the active session token from your device.
  • FCM Token Invalidation: Device push tokens are updated or deregistered whenever an account session ends.
  • Right of Erasure: Users may request account and associated profile removal by contacting their designated system administrator.
