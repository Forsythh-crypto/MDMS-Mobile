Official Security & Compliance Privacy Policy

Document Management System

Version 2.1 · Last Updated: September 14, 2026

1 Introduction

Welcome to the Document Management System ("we," "our," or "us") mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile companion services.

The Document Management System is an enterprise and institutional companion app designed to deliver real-time push notifications, secure document tracking, and routing updates directly to authorized personnel.

By creating an account or using the application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2 Information We Collect

We collect only the data essential to operate the system securely and deliver alerts.

a. Authentication Information

  • Email Address & User ID — Used to authenticate your account and control access.
  • Password — Transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS to our authentication endpoint. Passwords are never stored on your physical device.

b. Device Identifiers

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Token — A unique device token generated by Google Firebase, used solely to route incoming document notifications to your mobile device.

c. Session & Application State

  • Encrypted Session Token — Stored locally on your device via secure storage to maintain seamless access without requiring you to log in on every launch.
  • Notification Metadata — Document reference numbers, titles, timestamps, and status payloads dispatched by the backend server.

3 How We Use Information

We process collected information strictly for operational and security purposes:

  • Access Control — Verifying credentials against the authorized personnel directory.
  • Real-Time Alerts — Dispatching push notifications when documents are assigned, received, or acted upon.
  • Session Integrity — Securing access sessions and validating API requests.
  • Document Lifecycle Tracking — Supporting authorized timeline inspection and audit compliance.

🛡️ Zero Advertising / Analytics Policy: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or use your data for commercial advertising, behavioral tracking, or data profiling.

4 Third-Party Service Providers

We do not share your information with third-party vendors except where essential to provide the service:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — Used to dispatch cloud notifications. Firebase handles device registration tokens under Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

We do not permit any third party to use your information for their own independent purposes.

5 Data Storage & Security Standards

  • All network communications are transmitted exclusively over industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Local authentication credentials are encrypted using the secure Android Keystore or iOS Keychain.
  • Account credentials and document payloads reside on protected enterprise database servers, accessible only to authorized system administrators.
  • Access to backend systems is restricted through role-based permissions and audit logging.

6 Data Retention & Account Deletion

  • Device Cleanup — Logging out immediately purges the active session token from your device.
  • FCM Token Invalidation — Device push tokens are updated or deregistered whenever an account session ends.
  • Retention Period — Notification metadata and document tracking records are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill audit, compliance, and operational requirements, consistent with applicable institutional record-retention policies.
  • Right of Erasure — Users may request removal of their account and associated profile data by contacting their designated system administrator.

7 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and institutional policy, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to legal or audit retention obligations.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

To exercise any of these rights, contact your designated system administrator or the support contact listed in Section 10.

8 Children's Privacy

This application is intended solely for use by authorized personnel of institutional and enterprise organizations. It is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect information from, individuals under the age of 18.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other operational factors. Material changes will be communicated through the application or via official institutional channels, and the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be revised accordingly. Continued use of the application after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
